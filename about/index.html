I’m a first-year student on the Global BBA at ESSEC, in Paris. I’m Italian, and I work in Italian, English and French.

Family and friendship are central to who I am. I care deeply about the people close to me, and I believe in relationships built on trust, honesty and showing up for one another. That care for people also shapes what I hope to contribute through my work.

I’m studying business, and I’m exploring AI alongside it — reading about what new models can do, and about the questions that come with them. I haven’t settled on an industry or a career destination, and I’d rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise. This is the year for finding out.

My ambition is to build something with a purpose I believe in: a business or project whose success includes the good it does for people. I want to help create a future in which more people can live well and find happiness. As I learn and develop, I want to turn that ambition into practical work, alongside people who care about the difference they make.

That is where I find hope: in what we can do for one another, and in the possibility of turning care into lasting change.
